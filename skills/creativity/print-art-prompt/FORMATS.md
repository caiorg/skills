# Formats — production specs & composition rules

Reference for [`print-art-prompt`](SKILL.md), step 3. Read only the chosen format's section.

## Playmat (TCG)

**Production specs**
- 24×14 in (610×355 mm), aspect ratio 24:14 (12:7), horizontal
- optimized for 7200×4200 resolution (300 DPI)
- suitable for neoprene cloth printing
- safe composition margins; no important details near edges
- darkened outer edges for practical tabletop use
- high contrast focal readability

**Composition rules**
- central focal area clean and readable
- important subjects positioned within safe area
- edges less visually dense to avoid print cropping issues
- balanced negative space for cards during gameplay
- visual flow from left to right
- composition remains readable even when partially covered by cards

## Mousepad / desk mat

Ask which size when unstated; default to XL desk mat.

**Production specs**
- XL desk mat: 900×400 mm, aspect ratio 9:4, horizontal, optimized for 10800×4800 resolution (300 DPI)
- Standard mousepad: 250×210 mm, aspect ratio ~6:5, optimized for 3000×2520 resolution (300 DPI)
- suitable for cloth-surface sublimation printing
- safe composition margins; no important details near edges
- slightly darkened edges to hide wear and stitched borders

**Composition rules**
- XL: primary focal area on the left-to-center; the right third stays calmer (mouse traffic covers it); keyboard footprint tolerated at center-bottom
- Standard: single centered focal subject, readable at a glance
- moderate texture density so cursor tracking stays comfortable
- composition remains attractive when partially covered by peripherals

## Poster

Ask which size and orientation when unstated; default to 24×36 in portrait.

**Production specs**
- 24×36 in (610×914 mm), aspect ratio 2:3, portrait — or 18×24 in / A2 on request
- optimized for 7200×10800 resolution (300 DPI)
- full-bleed artwork with 3 mm bleed allowance on all sides
- suitable for matte or semi-gloss paper printing

**Composition rules**
- strong central or rule-of-thirds focal hierarchy
- readable both at wall-viewing distance and up close
- edges may carry detail, but keep critical elements inside the bleed-safe area
- vertical visual flow guiding the eye top to bottom
